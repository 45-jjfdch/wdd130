White Water Rafting Company

Our mission at White Water Rafting Company is simple: to provide the most thrilling and safest rafting adventures in the West. We believe that nature has the power to rejuvenate the soul, and we are passionate about sharing that experience with our customers. Join us for an unforgettable adventure!

History

Founded in 1985 by Captain John Oar, White Water Rafting Company. started as a small family business with a single inflatable raft and a dream. John believed everyone deserved to experience the rush of the river. Over the decades, we've grown, but our core values of safety, excitement, and love for the outdoors remain unchanged. Today, we operate on three different rivers and have guided thousands of adventurers through the white water.
